01CARE STARTS WITH A CONVERSATION

You deserve to feel heard and at ease.

It can feel hard to talk about a change in your body, a worry during pregnancy, or a question about periods. Dr. Anirudh wants you to feel safe sharing what is on your mind. He will listen, explain things in simple words and help you understand the next step.

05ONLINE CONSULTATION

Speak from home when an online visit fits.

Online visits take place on Google Meet. A video visit may not be right for every concern; the doctor will let you know if an in-person check is needed.

ONLINE VISIT₹50015 minutes · Google Meet

How to request a time

  1. Send an appointment request on WhatsApp.
  2. Share your name and whether you prefer an online or clinic visit. Please keep private health details for your consultation.
  3. The clinic will confirm within 24 hours. If confirmed as online, the Google Meet details will be shared with you.
Request online consultation ↗

Need urgent help? WhatsApp and online appointments are not emergency services. For severe, sudden or worrying symptoms, go to the nearest emergency department or call 112 in India. Do not wait for a WhatsApp reply.
